msgvault

msgvault is a command-line application that archives your Gmail messages to a local database on your own computer, so you can search and keep your email offline.

What it does

  • Connects to your Google account using OAuth and requests read-only access to Gmail.
  • Downloads your messages and stores them locally on your device.
  • Lets you search and browse the local archive without a network connection.

Your data

All email data stays on your device. It is not sent to, stored by, or shared with the developer or any third party. See the Privacy Policy.
