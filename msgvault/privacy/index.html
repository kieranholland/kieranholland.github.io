msgvault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy applies to msgvault, a command-line application that archives your Gmail messages locally.

Data accessed

With your explicit consent through Google OAuth, msgvault requests read-only access to your Gmail account (message content, labels and metadata) and your account email address, solely to build your local archive.

How data is used and stored

Downloaded data and OAuth tokens are stored only on your own device. msgvault does not transmit your Google user data to the developer or any other server, and does not use it for advertising, analytics, or training of AI/ML models. No human reads your data.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. The only network communication is between your device and Google's APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

msgvault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete the local archive and tokens at any time, and revoke msgvault's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/wesm/msgvault.
